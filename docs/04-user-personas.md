# Subtitle Studio — User Personas

**Document:** 04 — User Personas  
**Status:** Initial hypotheses; validate through research  
**Version:** 0.1

---

## 1. Purpose

Describe the initial user groups whose needs should guide product decisions. These are provisional personas, not research findings about verified individuals.

## 2. Persona A — The Viewer

**Goal:** Find subtitles that match a movie or episode, in a language they understand.

**Needs**
- Reliable search.
- Correct film/episode and release match.
- Clear language and version labels.
- Safe, trustworthy downloads.
- Transparent licence and provenance information.

**Pain points to validate**
- Wrong subtitle timing.
- Incorrect release match.
- Poor translation quality.
- Broken or misleading downloads.

**Potential success measure:** successful subtitle discovery and download.

## 3. Persona B — The Subtitle Contributor

**Goal:** Upload, correct and share subtitle files.

**Needs**
- Simple import and editing.
- Validation before upload.
- Contributor attribution.
- Version history.
- Clear licence choices.

**Potential success measure:** accepted contributions and repeat contributions.

## 4. Persona C — The Translator / Reviewer

**Goal:** Translate dialogue accurately and review AI-generated localization.

**Needs**
- Original dialogue and context.
- Adjacent dialogue visibility.
- Language variety and style controls.
- Efficient correction workflow.
- Review status and attribution.

**Potential success measure:** approved segments and low correction/rejection rates.

## 5. Persona D — The Independent Filmmaker

**Goal:** Make authorized video content available to additional language audiences.

**Needs**
- Project workspace.
- Subtitle and dubbing workflows.
- Collaborator access.
- Preview and export.
- Rights and delivery tracking.

**Potential success measure:** completed localization projects.

## 6. Persona E — The Content Creator

**Goal:** Produce accessible and multilingual versions of online video content.

**Needs**
- Fast subtitle generation.
- Editable translations.
- Repeatable workflows.
- Cost visibility.
- Suitable output formats.

**Potential success measure:** time from upload to approved export.

## 7. Persona F — The Localization Team

**Goal:** Coordinate multiple languages, translators, reviewers and deliverables.

**Needs**
- Project roles and permissions.
- Task assignment.
- Review queues.
- Version history.
- Consistent terminology and voice assignment.

**Potential success measure:** on-time delivery and review quality.

## 8. Research plan

Validate personas through interviews and usability sessions. Ask participants to demonstrate their current workflow rather than relying only on hypothetical preferences.

Record:
- User's actual task.
- Current tools and workarounds.
- Frequency and importance.
- Time and cost.
- Main failure points.
- Evidence of willingness to adopt or pay.

## 9. Open questions

- Which persona has the strongest immediate need for the first post-MVP feature?
- Are subtitle contributors also likely to be translators?
- What level of technical experience should the editor assume?
- Which users need local/offline processing?
