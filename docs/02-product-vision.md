# Subtitle Studio — Product Vision

**Document:** 02 — Product Vision  
**Status:** Draft for review  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 7 November 2026

---

## 1. Vision statement

Make video content easier to understand, create and share across languages by providing accessible subtitle and AI-assisted media-localization tools.

## 2. Long-term ambition

Subtitle Studio aims to become a multilingual media-localization platform serving viewers, subtitle contributors, translators, independent filmmakers, content creators and localization teams.

The platform should support both written localization (subtitles) and spoken localization (AI dubbing).

## 3. Mission

Provide practical tools that help people create, improve, discover and distribute accurate subtitles and localized audio, with particular attention to African languages that may be underserved by mainstream speech technologies.

## 4. Product pillars

1. **Subtitle Library:** discover, contribute, review and download subtitles.
2. **Subtitle Studio:** create, edit, synchronize, translate and export subtitles.
3. **AI Dubbing Studio:** convert dialogue into spoken target-language audio using controllable AI voice workflows.
4. **Community and Filmmaker Workspace:** coordinate translation, review and localization projects.

## 5. Product positioning

Subtitle Studio is not only a subtitle download website or a single-purpose subtitle editor. Its proposed differentiation is the connection between subtitle discovery, editing, translation, dubbing and collaborative review.

This positioning is a strategic hypothesis and should be validated through user research and competitor analysis.

## 6. Strategic principles

- Build a useful, focused product before expanding its scope.
- Treat African-language quality as a core product concern.
- Separate transcription, translation, alignment and speech generation.
- Keep AI output editable and reviewable.
- Make provider integrations replaceable.
- Design for rights-respecting media processing.
- Make processing costs and limitations understandable.
- Expand language coverage based on measured capability, not marketing claims.

## 7. Long-term success indicators

Potential measures include:

- Successful subtitle edits and exports.
- Subtitle match and correction rates.
- Search success and download completion.
- Translation acceptance and correction rates.
- Native-speaker ratings of speech naturalness and intelligibility.
- Dubbing completion and regeneration rates.
- Processing cost per media minute.
- Contributor retention and reviewed contributions.

Targets should be set only after baseline data is collected.

## 8. What the product is not initially

The first release is not a streaming service, a movie-hosting platform, a marketplace, a general-purpose video editor or an automatic full-film dubbing service.

## 9. Strategic sequencing

1. Validate the subtitle-editing workflow.
2. Add durable subtitle project management when needed.
3. Introduce transcription and translation as separate capabilities.
4. Evaluate Twi speech generation with native speakers.
5. Build a reviewed multi-speaker dubbing workflow.
6. Expand language coverage and community capabilities.

## 10. Open decisions

- Which user segment should be prioritized after the editor MVP?
- What is the first sustainable business model?
- Which language should follow Twi?
- Which features should remain free and which may require paid processing?
- What level of professional verification will the product offer?
